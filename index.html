Catch

Every bug has a story.

Point the camera at a bug and watch BugJug guess what it is as you get closer.

  • Meet your bug: its name, how rare it is, and a "Did you know?" fact.
  • Your pet is hand-drawn with colours taken from the real bug, plus its own name and personality.

Care

Little friends on your shelf.

Pat your bugs, feed them and give them toys. They get lonely, hungry and bored, but nothing bad ever happens. They're just happy to see you.

  • Dress them up in hats and celebrate their birthdays.
  • Grow your friendship one heart at a time.
  • Bugs of the same kind share a jar, and every original photo is kept in their field notes.
